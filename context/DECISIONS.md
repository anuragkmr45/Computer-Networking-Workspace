## Unsettled, but not blockers
OS and lab environment; time budget; exact videos completed; specific certification sitting; access to a lab VM or paid sandbox. Ask only when the answer is necessary for the immediate task. Do not re-ask known career goals.

## Rule for future changes
Record the request, affected files, rationale, and approval for substantial changes. Do not let a source refresh silently rewrite the learner's goal or previous evidence.
