## When an exam becomes a real target
Verify the official current blueprint and effective dates; record exact objective IDs and version; map each objective to lesson, practice, platform/version, and evidence; list uncovered topics explicitly. Check eligibility and exam logistics separately. Reverify before booking rather than relying on this snapshot.
